

# 🚲 Project 3 Bob's Bicycle Rental – Power BI Dashboard 
![Bob's Bicycle Rental Main Dashboard](../images/Bike%20Main%20Dashboard.png))

## 📌 Project Summary

This project delivers an interactive **Power BI dashboard** for **Bob's Bicycle Rental**, built to analyze and visualize business performance metrics. The database was created using SQL Server and connected to PowerBI. The dashboard highlights:

* **Hourly Revenue Analysis**
* **Profit and Revenue Trends**
* **Seasonal Revenue**
* **Rider Demographics**

The goal is to support informed decision-making, identify profitable time periods, and provide recommendations for business growth.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights from the Dashboard

### 1. **Hourly Revenue Analysis**

* Mid-day to early evening (10:00 – 15:00 hrs) shows the **highest earnings**.
* Wednesdays and Fridays stand out with **notably higher sales** compared to other weekdays.

### 2. **Profit & Revenue Trends (KPI over Time)**

* Revenue and ridership show a **clear seasonal pattern**, peaking in **mid-year (spring to summer)** and dipping in winter months.
* Overall revenue: **\$15M**
* Overall profit: **\$10.45M**
* Riders: **3M**
* Profit margin: **0.45**

### 3. **Seasonal Revenue**

* **Autumn:** \$4.9M (highest seasonal revenue)
* **Summer:** \$4.2M
* **Winter:** \$3.9M
* **Spring:** \$2.2M (lowest seasonal revenue)

### 4. **Rider Demographics**

* **Registered Riders:** 81.17% (majority)
* **Casual Riders:** 18.83%

---

## 💡 Recommendations

1. **Dynamic Pricing Strategy**

   * Leverage higher demand in **Autumn and Summer** with slight price increases.
   * Offer discounts or promotions during **Spring**, the lowest revenue season, to attract more riders.

2. **Operational Optimization**

   * Increase bike availability on **Wednesdays, Fridays, and mid-day to evening hours**, where demand is strongest.
   * Streamline costs during low-demand hours and off-peak seasons.

3. **Rider Engagement**

   * Encourage **casual riders (18.83%)** to convert into long-term registered members through subscription incentives.
   * Focus marketing campaigns around the **3M strong rider base**, tailoring seasonal offers.
